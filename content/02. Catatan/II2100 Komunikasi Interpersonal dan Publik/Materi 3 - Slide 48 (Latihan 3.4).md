# Task: Listening

## ACTIVITY 3.4: Willingness to Listen Measure

> **Instructions:** The following twenty-four statements refer to listening. Please indicate the degree to which each statement applies to you by marking whether you:
> 
> **1 = Strongly Disagree; 
> 2 = Disagree; 
> 3 = Neutral; 
> 4 = Agree; 
> 5 = Strongly Agree**

- \[2] **1.** I dislike listening to boring people.
    
- \[3] **2.** Generally, I can listen to a boring person.
    
- \[3] **3.** I am bored and tired while listening to a boring conversation.
    
- \[4] **4.** I will listen when the content of a conversation is boring.
    
- \[2] **5.** Listening to boring people about boring content makes me tired, sleepy, and bored.

### SCORING:

Scores can range from 24 to 120. To compute the score on this instrument, complete the following steps:

Step 1: Add scores for items 2, 4, 6, 8, 9, 12, 14, 16, 17, 19, 21, and 23

$$3 + 4 + 4 + 2 + 4 + 4 + 5 + 4 + 3 + 3 + 5 + 2 = 43$$

Step 2: Add scores for items 1, 3, 5, 7, 10, 11, 13, 15, 18, 20, 22, and 24

$$2 + 3 + 2 + 4 + 2 + 2 + 2 + 2 + 3 + 3 + 1 + 5 = 31$$

Step 3: Total score = 72 - Total from Step 1 + Total from Step 2.
Scores above 89 indicate a high willingness to listen. Scores below 59 indicate a low willingness to listen. Scores between 59 and 89 indicate a moderate willingness to listen.

> $$ Score = 72 - 43 + 31 = 60$$
